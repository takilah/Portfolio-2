Disclosure

This project is part of an active FSAE competition program. The team has asked that renders, CFD imagery, and design detail stay unpublished for now, given how directly aero performance affects competitiveness against other teams. The figures below will be added once the team is comfortable releasing them.

Objective

Hit the downforce target the team has set for this iteration, while working within the packaging space left available by the car's chassis, suspension, and powertrain designs. Every surface has to clear the FSAE 2027 rules handbook, aerodynamics regulations under Section T.7.
