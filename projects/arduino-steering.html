Arduino Steering
and Throttle Control

Designed and fabricated a custom PVC chassis and front-axle steering system in Fusion 360, then integrated a servo and high-torque DC motors with an Arduino running C++ steering and throttle logic.

Timeframe
2025
Team

Role
Designer
Scope
PVC chassis, front-axle steering system, servo and DC motor integration, control logic
Constraints

Tools
Fusion 360, Arduino, C++
Methods
>>> How steering and throttle logic were programmed and tuned
Status
Functional steering and throttle control via Arduino-driven servo and DC motors
