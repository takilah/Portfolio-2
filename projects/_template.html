Objective

>>> Two or three sentences on the actual constraint, not the assignment. What was underperforming, what it cost the car, and which number you were trying to move. State the operating point and the units.

>>> If a rule or standard bounded the design, name it. Readers in your industry recognize those citations, and they establish that you worked inside real limits rather than an open-ended exercise.

Approach

>>> How you decided, not everything you did. Which load case or ride height you chose and why, what you modeled versus what you measured, and where the model disagreed with reality.

>>> Include one decision you got wrong and corrected. It costs nothing and it is the strongest credibility signal on a page like this. Engineers trust people who report their own revisions.

Result

>>> Measured outcomes with units, and the conditions they were measured under. If the result missed the target, say by how much and what you learned. A quantified miss reads better than a vague success.
